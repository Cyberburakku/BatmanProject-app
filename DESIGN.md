# Visual Style Guide — Jay Flow Compression

## Colours

- **Primary colour:** Electric Blue — `#00A3FF`
- **Secondary colour:** Fire Orange — `#FF6B00`
- **Accent colour** (buttons and highlights only): Gold — `#FFD700`
- **Background colour:** Deep Navy — `#0A0E1A`
- **Text colour:** White — `#FFFFFF`

## Fonts

- **Headings:** Bebas Neue — tall, bold, poster-style letters that feel fast and powerful, like a sports jersey number.
- **Body text:** Inter — clean and easy to read, so the site still feels calm and trustworthy under all that energy.

Together they suit the feeling because Bebas Neue brings the punch and Inter keeps it readable.

## Overall feel

Driven, electric, lively.

## Rule

Use these exact values in everything you build for me unless I say otherwise.
