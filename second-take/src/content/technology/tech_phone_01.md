## Introduction

A phone connects you to job applications, appointments, and the people in your corner. Getting comfortable with the basics now means one less thing to figure out under pressure later.

## Content

Setting up a new phone starts with turning it on and following the prompts for language, Wi-Fi connection, and account creation. Most phones ask you to create or sign into an email account first, since this connects your contacts, photos, and apps together in one place. Take it one screen at a time, and skip anything that asks for payment information until you're ready to add it later.

## Activity

Write down one email address you already have, or one you plan to create this week. Write down one person's phone number you want to save as your first contact.

## Encouragement

Getting your phone set up is one task completely in your control today.
