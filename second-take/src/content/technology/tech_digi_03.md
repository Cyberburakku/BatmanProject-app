## Introduction

The internet holds real opportunity, and it also holds real risk if you don't know what to watch for. This lesson gives you the basics to protect yourself.

## Content

A strong password uses a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols, and it stays different across your important accounts like email and banking. Scam messages often create urgency, asking you to click a link immediately or share personal information right away, so slowing down before clicking anything unfamiliar protects you. Never share a password, social security number, or bank information with anyone who reaches out to you first by text, email, or phone call.

## Activity

Write down one password idea using a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols, without using anything easy to guess like a birthday.

## Encouragement

Protecting your information online is one more way you're taking care of your own future.
