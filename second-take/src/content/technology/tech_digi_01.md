## Introduction

Email is how most employers, programs, and services stay in touch with you. Knowing how to check it, write it, and organize it puts you back in control of your own communication.

## Content

An email works like a digital letter: you write a message, add the recipient's address at the top, and press send. Checking email regularly matters, since job offers, appointment reminders, and program updates often arrive there first. Keeping your inbox organized by deleting messages you don't need, and starring the ones you do, makes it easier to find what matters later.

## Activity

Write a short practice email to yourself, including a subject line, one sentence of body text, and your name at the bottom.

## Encouragement

Every email you send is one more way you're staying connected to what's ahead.
