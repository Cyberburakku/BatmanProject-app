## Introduction

A computer can feel unfamiliar if it's been a while, and that's completely normal. This lesson covers the basics you'll actually use.

## Content

Every computer works off a few consistent basics: a mouse or trackpad to point and click, a keyboard to type, and a desktop screen showing icons for the programs you use most. Opening a program means clicking its icon twice, and closing one means clicking the X in the corner. Saving your work regularly protects it from getting lost, and most programs save automatically if you look for a small "saved" message near the top of the screen.

## Activity

Practice opening one program and closing it again, three times in a row, until the motion feels familiar.

## Encouragement

Every skill on a computer becomes easier the second time you do it.
