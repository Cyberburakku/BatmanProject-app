## Introduction

Most job searches now start online, and knowing how to search well saves real time and reduces frustration.

## Content

Job search sites let you filter by location, job type, and pay range, narrowing results down to what actually fits your life right now. A strong application usually needs a resume uploaded as a file, and sometimes a short cover letter explaining why you're a fit. Applying to multiple positions in one sitting, rather than one at a time over several days, builds momentum and keeps the process from feeling endless.

## Activity

Search one job title you're interested in, and write down two things you notice: one requirement you already meet, and one you want to work toward.

## Encouragement

Every application you send is a real step toward the work you're building.
