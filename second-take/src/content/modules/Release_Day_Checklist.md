## Introduction

This is the last lesson on your path, and it pulls everything together into one list you can hold in your hand on day one.

## Content

Release day moves fast, and decisions are harder to make well when everything is new at once. A checklist written ahead of time carries the thinking you already did here. Work through the list below and tick off what is true. Anything left unticked is not a failure, it is just your next task, and the resources in this app cover most of them. Print this or write it out on paper before your date. Paper does not need a charged battery, a signal, or a password you cannot remember under pressure.

## Activity

Tick off what is already handled, and write the unticked items on a piece of paper to keep on you.

## Encouragement

You built this path one lesson at a time, and you are walking out with a plan. That is real work, and it was yours.
